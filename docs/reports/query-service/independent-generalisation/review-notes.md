# Independent evaluation review: durable findings

An independent architecture-plan-auditor reviewed the frozen construction,
oracles, collector, public contracts and evidence metadata. It did not inspect
the original26 scorecard, main/regression responses, or mutate application/data.
Its follow-up reviewed support admissibility for H21/H22/H44 without seeing the
actual responses. Review is a separate challenge, not an implementation gate.

## Accepted findings

- Frozen question/oracle hash chain and 60/48 denominator arithmetic were sound.
  Counts use exact resource identities, not membership-row counts. H10 and H49
  evidence guards independently justify their predeclared blocked status.
- All expected facts describe the recorded local RDF slice, not complete world
  truth. Office episodes must stay qualified; no extra historical tenure may be
  invented from general knowledge. Names are display labels; IRIs ground joins.
- The 48-case support classification overclaims three compositions. H21/H22
  require historical date-to-term-to-constituency selection not enumerated by the
  numbered/current-term membership projection. H44 adds numbered-term scope to
  the closed office-anchor projection. Preserve frozen expectations and primary
  48-case result; report an explicit 45-case admissibility sensitivity. Do not
  promote correct refusals on these cases to factual passes.
- H44's oracle is a case-specific positive selection, not a general-purpose
  implementation of a four-way interval intersection evaluator. Its conjunction
  of overlap inequalities is not complete for arbitrary intervals. The local
  Michael Ring result is not a license to reuse that algebra as acceptance
  authority for other cases. H44 is excluded from the supported sensitivity
  independently of this limitation.
- The collector's outcome attribute and HTTP status are not factual scoring.
  Compare complete sets/values and inspect requested constraints/qualifications.
  Candidate identities do not become answers, and unsupported gender/election
  clauses must never disappear into a simpler selection.
- Report both outcome consistency and factual content/answer availability. Stable
  refusals can make an inaccurate system look very repeatable. Do not turn a
  later successful rerun into the original score.

## Verification conditions discharged

- The byte-preserving gzip of `oracle-context.json` decompresses to its frozen
  SHA-256; artifact compression is not RDF mutation.
- The post-main context check and final verification matched the pre-call
  census: **7,982 named graphs, 1,119,860 triples**, the same 65-term chronology,
  and the same 19 Murphy candidate identities.
- Final verification also repeated **all 50 factual evidence queries and both
  evidence guards**: every returned value set matched its frozen counterpart.
  No source/template hashes changed. Census equality is not a cryptographic
  whole-dataset content digest; that residual limitation is retained.
- No Member-IRI/name cardinality discrepancy occurs in the selected frozen sets.
  This does not authorize deduplication by name in the service.
- H41/H42 support is directly documented by the executable public contract's
  `dail_seats` / `dail_parties_and_groups` families; no additional exclusions were
  made merely because the semantic fallback denied them.

## Independence and scope qualifications

The source diff exposed specifically **Dublin** Central/Dublin South-Central
preposition tests and one previous-Dáil constituency lookup, not original26
answers or a full shared-constituency campaign. The reviewer withdrew an initial
concern about Cork South-Central/H24 after that clarification. H30 retains a
reduced-independence note because relative-term wording was exposed. The public
guide also contains development examples, so this is contract-informed testing,
not fully blind language sampling. No questions/expectations were changed after
freeze to resolve any review finding.
